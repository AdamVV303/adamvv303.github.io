SAMPLE APPROACH / CONTENT STRATEGY

Useful content starts
with a real question.

My starting point: identify what the reader needs, decide what the page should accomplish, and define how to evaluate it.

1. Define the audience and goal

Specify who the page is for and what they should be able to understand or do after reading it. Connect that purpose to a relevant business outcome.

2. Map the question to a page

Review existing content before creating something new. Decide whether to improve a current page, combine overlapping content, or address a missing topic.

3. Write a focused brief

Include the page purpose, main question, supporting questions, evidence needed, internal links, and a relevant next step. Use a descriptive title and headings that help readers scan.

4. Publish and document

Check links, mobile readability, image descriptions, and indexability. Record the publication date and starting measurements.

5. Review the evidence

Look at relevant impressions, clicks, engagement, and inquiries. Note other changes that may affect results before attributing an improvement to the content update.
