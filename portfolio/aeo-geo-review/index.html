SAMPLE FRAMEWORK / NOT A CLIENT CASE STUDY

AEO & GEO review.

A repeatable way to turn questions about AI search visibility into a scoped review and an actionable plan.

Start with a defined question

Choose a business topic, audience, and set of pages. Record the questions the audience asks and the decisions they need to make.

Review four areas

  1. Access: Can search engines reach the important content? Check indexability, internal links, canonical URLs, and the sitemap.
  2. Clarity: Does the page explain its topic directly? Look for descriptive headings, clear definitions, and complete answers.
  3. Evidence: Are important claims supported by attributable sources, firsthand examples, or documented results?
  4. Identity: Can readers tell who created the content, what the organization does, and how to verify the information?

Turn findings into priorities

For each finding, record the page, evidence, proposed change, owner, and a way to evaluate the result. Prioritize issues that block access or confuse readers before minor presentation changes.

Measure without overclaiming

Keep a dated record of the exact prompts, platforms, and cited URLs used in AI observations. Compare those observations with search visibility and identifiable referral visits. An isolated citation is an observation, not proof of consistent visibility or business impact.

This framework is a sample methodology. No client performance results or AI citation guarantees are implied.
